READ FIRST - SCOPE AND METHOD
- Only https://cit.edu.in/ pages and official documents linked from them were used.
- Content is reproduced as published. Obvious formatting glitches were corrected without changing facts.
- Where information was not found: "Not found in the inspected official sources."
- Where official pages disagree, both values are recorded and flagged "CONFLICT".
- This file does NOT claim coverage of the entire website. See Section 18 (Source Inventory) for exactly which pages and PDFs were inspected and which were not.
- Primary academic year in the sources: 2026-27 (admission forms, fee tables, hostel tariff, brochures titled 2026). Some pages carry 2025-26 data; these are labelled.

SECTION 2. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY vs PROGRAMME-SPECIFIC ELIGIBILITY - HOW THIS FILE IS ORGANISED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- General eligibility = rules applying to a whole category of programmes (e.g., "UG Engineering" minimum qualifications) - Section 3.
- Programme-specific eligibility = rules stated for a named programme (e.g., M.Sc. DCS alternative subject route, MCA, MBA, M.E., Part-Time B.E., Ph.D.) - Sections 4 to 7.
- Government counselling norms (TNEA / DoTE / Anna University) are referenced by CIT but are NOT reproduced on the inspected CIT pages beyond what is quoted below.

SECTION 3. UNDERGRADUATE (B.E. / B.Tech.) - GENERAL ELIGIBILITY AND PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: https://cit.edu.in/admission/admission-requirements (academic year not stated on this page)

3.1 Minimum qualifications - UG Programmes (Engineering)
- A pass in HSC (Academic) with Maths, Physics and Chemistry (OR)
- A pass in HSC (Vocational Stream) with Engineering Vocational subjects and any one or two of related subjects (Maths, Physics and Chemistry) (OR)
- Diploma in one of the Engineering Courses (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical or Electronics) (OR)
- Any other Equivalent qualification approved by the State Board of Technical Education / Higher Secondary Board of Tamilnadu.
- Candidates belonging to communities other than SC/ST should have passed the qualifying examination in not more than two appearances. Candidates belonging to SC/ST should have passed the qualifying examination in not more than three appearances.

3.2 Minimum marks (as published)
- "For SC/ST candidates, a mere pass in the qualifying examination will suffice."
- Candidates belonging to communities other than SC/ST should have obtained the following minimum percentage of marks:
 A) H.S.C. (Academic) and other equivalent candidates:
   OC - 45% average in the related subjects
   BC - 40% average in the related subjects
   MBC & DNC - 40% average in the related subjects
   SC/ST - 40% average in the related subjects
 B) H.S.C. (Vocational) candidates: OC 45%; BC 40%; MBC & DNC 40%; SC/ST 40% (average in the related subjects)
 C) Diploma holders: OC 45%; BC 40%; MBC & DNC 40%; SC/ST 40% (average in the related subjects)
 CONFLICT (within the same page): the page first says a mere pass suffices for SC/ST candidates, and then lists "SC/ST - 40%" in each category table. Not resolved; see Section 16.
- "Related subjects" are not defined on the inspected page beyond the HSC subject list above.

3.3 Age limit
- "No Age Limit" (https://cit.edu.in/admission/admission-requirements).

3.4 Selection route (B.E./B.Tech. first year)
- Single Window Counseling by DoTE, Chennai (https://cit.edu.in/admission/admissions-details).
- The page https://cit.edu.in/admission/under-graduate describes admission "through TNEA counselling" in its search-engine description; the page body states "students are allocated to their specific branches during the initial counseling process". The admission paperwork refers to "TNEA Application No." (Aided call letter PDF).
- Minimum cut-off marks, rank cut-offs or seat matrix: Not found in the inspected official sources.

3.5 Programme descriptions on the UG page (https://cit.edu.in/admission/under-graduate)
- B.E. (full-time, eight semesters): Civil; Mechanical; Electrical & Electronics; Electronics & Communication; Computer Science and Engineering; Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology).
- B.Tech. (full-time): Chemical Engineering (eight semesters); Information Technology; Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.
- Part-Time B.E. (eight semesters, for Engineering Diploma holders): Mechanical, Civil, Electrical & Electronics.
- Programme-specific subject or marks requirements for individual B.E./B.Tech. branches (beyond Section 3.1/3.2): Not found in the inspected official sources.

3.6 B.E./B.Tech. admission process at CIT (Aided call letter and checklist PDF, academic year 2026-27)
Source: https://cit.edu.in/uploads/epdf_documents/9d5b2df7049df80cc599e8f75bf25faa.pdf (linked as "Download Application (Aided)" on https://cit.edu.in/admission/first-year-be-btech-admissions)
- A seat is "provisionally allotted"; admission "is purely provisional and shall be confirmed only after: (1) Verification of all original certificates; (2) Establishment of your eligibility for admission; (3) Approval by the Directorate of Technical Education (DoTE), Government of Tamil Nadu, and Anna University, Chennai."
- Candidates report on the scheduled date for certificate verification; reporting time options on the template: 9.00 a.m. / 11.00 a.m. / 2.00 p.m.; venue: Auditorium, Coimbatore Institute of Technology. (Date left blank in the template.)
- Admission fee is paid at the time of admission "only by UPI payment / Debit, credit cards / Internet banking."
- "Failure to report on the scheduled date and time with the required documents and prescribed fees may result in forfeiture of the admission opportunity."
- False information: forfeiture of admission at any stage; debarment from pursuing studies for three years; legal action (as stated in the document).

SECTION 5. FIVE-YEAR INTEGRATED M.Sc. PROGRAMMES

Brochure 2026 (https://cit.edu.in/uploads/generalfiles/1777391787_Updated_MSc_BROCHURE.pdf) ; M.Sc. Admission Brochure (https://cit.edu.in/uploads/epdf_documents/f7bccfc900ef6e3f02902e14643a91f5.pdf)
Offered by the Department of Computing. Approved intake: 60 seats each. The M.Sc. Brochure and the M.Sc. Admission Brochure PDF contain identical text.

5.1 Eligibility - M.Sc. Software Systems (SS) / Data Science (DS) / Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML)
- A pass in HSC (or equivalent) examination with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as subjects is required.
- Candidates (other than SC/ST) must have a minimum aggregate of 70% in Mathematics + Physics + (Chemistry or Computer Science).
- Note on the page: this does not mean 70% in each subject individually; the requirement is based on the total combined marks. Example given: out of 300 marks (100 per subject), a minimum of 210 marks in total.
- The website page 5-year integrated page and the brochures state the 70% aggregate applies to candidates "other than SC/ST". A numeric minimum for SC/ST candidates: Not found in the inspected official sources (the "pass in HSC with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry" condition is stated for all).

5.2 Eligibility - M.Sc. Decision and Computing Sciences (DCS) (programme-specific; two routes)
Route 1:
- A pass in HSC (or equivalent) with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as subjects.
- Candidates (other than SC/ST) must have a minimum aggregate of 70% in Mathematics + Physics + (Chemistry or Computer Science).
Route 2 (OR):
- A pass in HSC / equivalent exam with one subject from Category A and two subjects from Category B as the subjects of study.
 CATEGORY A - Mathematics / Applied Mathematics / Statistics / Business Mathematics / Business Mathematics & Statistics.
 CATEGORY B - Commerce / Economics / Accountancy / Computer Science.
- Candidates (other than SC/ST) with an aggregate of 70% and above in the three subjects in total (one from Category A and two from Category B) only need to apply.
(The brochure describes DCS as "Ideal for Science & Commerce Students".)

5.3 Entrance examination and selection (stated on the M.Sc. page)
- Two-stage evaluation: (1) Higher Secondary Examination performance - marks in the qualifying board examination are considered; (2) Institute-Level Entrance Examination - eligible candidates must appear for an internal entrance test conducted by the institute.
- Note: "The examination center is located only in Coimbatore."
- Syllabus, pattern, duration or weightage of the entrance test or interview: Not found in the inspected official sources.

SECTION 12. FINANCIAL AID, FEE CONCESSIONS AND FIRST GRADUATE CONCESSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: https://cit.edu.in/admission/financial-aids (academic year not stated) and the Aided call-letter PDF.
- The page states that the institute facilitates "more than 24 government and private scholarship schemes" for undergraduate students.
- Guidelines: scholarship applications on the prescribed forms to the Principal's Office within 30 days of the institute reopening or the date of admission, whichever is later; all certificates and documents enclosed (incomplete submissions not considered); a student may receive only one scholarship or fee concession at a time; continuation subject to the minimum attendance percentage stipulated by the University; serious misconduct (including strikes or ragging) results in immediate cancellation of benefits.
- Government-sponsored schemes named: Adi Dravidar Welfare Scholarships (General and Higher Education Special); Backward Class (BC) Welfare Scholarships; Tamil Nadu Minority Merit-cum-Means Scholarship; Tamil Nadu Farmer Social Security Welfare Scholarship; Chief Minister's Scholarship and Arignar Anna Award; interstate scholarships (Pondicherry, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Meghalaya, Shillong, Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura, Nagaland, Mizoram); AICTE Pragathi Scheme (women students); Physically Handicapped Scholarship and AICTE Saksham Scheme (differently-abled students); scholarships for children of teachers, defense personnel, ex-servicemen and deceased government employees; Gandhi Memorial and Merit-cum-Means Scholarships.
- Mandatory fee concessions (as per Rule 92 - MER): students admitted under Government-Aided programmes are eligible for a full tuition fee waiver if they belong to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), or Converted Christians from SC/ST communities; "This concession is granted irrespective of the family's annual income."
- Private/trust-funded: Tamil Nadu Educational Trust; Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC); Siragugal Scholarship Scheme.
- Scholarship values, eligibility conditions and forms: "students are encouraged to visit the Institute Office." (Not published on the page.)
- First Graduate concession: a "First Graduate Concession - Declaration Form" is included in the Aided PDF. It requires TNEA/TNLEA application number, year of admission, college code, branch, First Graduate Certificate number, and details of family members (name, relationship, age, educational qualification, EMIS number of class 10th/11th/12th, whether ID proof attached). The declaration states liability for criminal prosecution and "return the amount equal to three time the tuition fee waived" if information is false. The concession's monetary scope is not stated in the PDF.
- Home page links "Scholarships" to https://cit.edu.in/students/scholarship (not opened).
