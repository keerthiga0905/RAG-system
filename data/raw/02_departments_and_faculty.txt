Overview (paraphrased from department home page):
- Department started in 1956. Postgraduate programme in Structural Engineering began in 1969; Ph.D. programme in Civil Engineering in 1978; PG programme in Environmental Engineering in 2005; PG programme in Construction Management in 2011.
- Faculty are described as experienced in academia and industry, with specialised areas listed as: Structural Analysis & Design, Structural Optimization, Concrete Technology, Steel Structures, Environmental Engineering, Construction Management, Transportation Engineering, Earthquake Engineering, Remote Sensing, Impact Assessment, and Prestressed Concrete Structures.

Mission (paraphrased): to impart quality education and the skills industry needs, with professional ethics and an attitude for innovation in Civil Engineering through state-of-the-art technology.

Abbreviation: The page's "Know More" link points to https://cit.edu.in/cse (URL slug only; no abbreviation stated in the text). Abbreviation otherwise not found in the inspected official sources.

Overview (paraphrased):
- Department established in 1986. It offers the B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering and a Ph.D. research programme across disciplines within computer science and engineering.
- Stated aim: to become a Centre of Excellence in Education and Research, linking Computer and Information Sciences with Natural Sciences and core engineering disciplines.
- A Project Development Cell (PDC) was set up in academic year 2024-2025 for real-world student projects. (The same page also calls it "Product Development Cell (PDC)" in an event item; naming inconsistency on the source page.)

Vision (paraphrased): to become a leading department in computer science and engineering education that nurtures globally competent professionals.
Mission (paraphrased, 3 points): (1) value-based technical education producing globally competent engineers who can deliver secure, "Out-of-the-Box" computing and IT solutions; (2) collaborative learning and research with industry and research organisations for knowledge transfer; (3) enabling graduates to adapt to changing technology with strong fundamentals.
