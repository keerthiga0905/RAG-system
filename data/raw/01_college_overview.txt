SECTION 2. ESTABLISHMENT AND INSTITUTIONAL HISTORY
Source: https://cit.edu.in/ ; https://cit.edu.in/about-us ; https://cit.edu.in/about-us/autonomous-status
====================================================================
Established: 1956 (homepage: "Established in 1956 by the V. Rangaswamy Naidu Educational Trust (VRET)").

History text (About Us page, "The Management"):
"Established in 1956 in Coimbatore, the V. Rangaswamy Naidu Educational Trust (VRET) is dedicated to advancing technical education in Tamil Nadu. Founded by visionary industrialists and philanthropists Prof. R. Venkataswamy Naidu and Prof. P. R. Ramakrishnan, the Trust launched the Coimbatore Institute of Technology (CIT) in 1956, followed by the CIT Sandwich Polytechnic College in 1961."

Leadership history as published:
- Prof. R. Venkataswamy Naidu served as Correspondent from 1956 to 1994.
- Prof. P. R. Ramakrishnan was Founder-Principal for 25 years and served as Correspondent for 12 years.
- Dr. S. R. K. Prasad took the helm as Managing Trustee in 2008, followed by Sri S. Rajiv Rangasami.

Former Correspondents panel (same page):
- Sri. R. Venkataswamy Naidu (1956 - 1994), B.Sc.(Tech) Manchester, Founder Correspondent
- Prof. P. R. Ramakrishnan (1994-2008), M.S.(M.I.T) USA, M.B.A., F.I.E., Founder Principal, Former Member of Parliament
- Dr. S. R. K. Prasad (2008-2021), D.Sc.(USA), FORSI, Managing Trustee, VRET Chairman, Governing Council, CIT

Autonomy:
- Homepage: autonomous since 1987.
- Autonomous Status page: autonomous system introduced "since the inception of the Autonomous scheme in the year 1987".
- Undated prospectus PDF (see Section 1 URL): "The Institute was granted autonomous status by Bharathiar University with effect from the academic year 1987-88."
Autonomous-scheme features published (https://cit.edu.in/about-us/autonomous-status): continuous assessment in laboratory practicals, workshop practice and engineering drawing since 2001; credit system for all programmes since 2001; Choice Based Credit System followed from 2015; four weeks of compulsory industrial training since 2009; syllabi revision every 4 years or when necessary; option to study German/French in II semester; grades awarded instead of marks; mini project of 2 credits in V and VI semesters; complete autonomy for conduct of PG programmes; revaluation option; special supplementary examination for outgoing batches; course "Science of Creativity and Professional Ethics" since 1987.
Office of the Controller of Examinations (as published on that page): administered by Dr. R. Sriram, Controller of Examinations, and Dr. R. Sathyanarayan Sridhar, Deputy Controller of Examinations, assisted by thirteen staff.

Homepage figures as published (claims, not independently verified): "We have 30K happy Students." Homepage counters labelled PLACED STUDENTS, PAPERS PUBLISHED and TOTAL FACULTY had no numeric values in the extracted text.

CONFLICT FLAGS (Section 2):
(a) Founding year of the Trust: About Us body text says VRET was "Established in 1956"; the caption under the Founder panel on the same page says "Established in 1955 by Philanthropists and Founder Trustees" (Sri. V. Rangaswamy Naidu, Founder Managing Trustee). Both recorded; not resolved.
(b) Prof. P. R. Ramakrishnan: body text says Correspondent for 12 years; Former Correspondents panel lists "1994-2008" (14 years). Both recorded; not resolved.
(c) Source of autonomy/affiliation: the prospectus PDF says autonomy was granted by Bharathiar University from 1987-88; the homepage says affiliated to Anna University. Likely historical vs current, but the pages do not state this; recorded as published.

====================================================================
Mission (About Us page):
"The mission of CIT is to 'impart high quality education and training to its students to make them World-Class Engineers with a foresight to the changes and problems, and pioneers to offer innovative solutions to benefit the nation and the world at large'."
(Homepage wording differs slightly in capitalisation: "Impart high quality education and training to its students to make them world-class engineers with a foresight to the changes and problems, and pioneers who offer innovative solutions to benefit the nation and the world at large".)

Vision (About Us page):
"The Institute strives to inculcate sound engineering knowledge along with a strong realization of social responsibilities, enabling students to address current and emerging national challenges and contribute their expertise to the global arena."
(Homepage wording: "The Institute strives to inculcate a sound knowledge in engineering along with a realization of social responsibilities to enable its students to combat the current and impending challenges faced by our country and to extend their expertise to the global arena.")

Core Values (About Us page):
1. Aiding Social Advancements through Technologies - technologies are instruments for promoting individuals and society; curricula, syllabi and teaching-learning methods aim at holistic progress and societal uplift.
2. Employability for Students - the Institution strives to mould students as qualified manpower through deeper knowledge, critical skills and transferable competencies.
3. Quality Education - ensured through quality teaching, state-of-the-art infrastructure, research and development, curricula in cutting-edge technologies, industry-institution interaction, a healthy academic ambience, co-curricular and extracurricular activities including internships.
4. Appreciation of Intellectual Excellence - intellectual accomplishments of students and faculty recognised; numerous endowment awards instituted.
5. Transparency, Accountability and Empathy - core values embedded in the administration.

Source: https://cit.edu.in/ ; https://cit.edu.in/about-us
====================================================================
Homepage description ("Developing Future-ready Graduates"):
"Established in 1956 by the V. Rangaswamy Naidu Educational Trust (VRET), Coimbatore Institute of Technology (CIT) stands as one of Tamil Nadu's most respected and sought-after institutions. An autonomous institution since 1987, CIT is proudly affiliated with Anna University, Chennai. Our vibrant 69-acre campus is home to 14 departments. We offer a wide range of programmes, from traditional engineering and cutting-edge digital technologies to five-year integrated M.Sc. degrees, MCA, and MBA programmes."

"CIT is defined by a legacy of excellence. Our teaching methods don't just follow trends, they prepare graduates to be future-ready, technically skilled, and socially responsible. This commitment is reflected in our exceptional placement record, with top-tier multinationals and innovative startups consistently recruiting CITians."
