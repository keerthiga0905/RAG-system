1. GENERAL SCHOLARSHIP POLICY & APPLICATION PROCEDURES
Source: https://cit.edu.in/students/scholarship

Application Procedure:
- Students wishing to apply for a scholarship must submit a duly completed application to the Office of the Principal.
- Applications should be filed within 30 days from the date the institute reopens or from the student's official date of admission, whichever is applicable.
- Applicants must attach all required documents and certificates that support their eligibility for the specific scholarship category. Incomplete applications may not be considered.

Eligibility Conditions and Continuation Criteria:
- Attendance Requirement: Students must maintain the minimum attendance percentage stipulated by the university.
- One Scholarship Policy: A student is eligible to receive only one scholarship or fee concession at a time.
- Disciplinary Conduct: Scholarships are granted based on trust and responsibility. Any form of misconduct, including involvement in ragging or participation in strikes, may result in immediate cancellation of financial assistance.

3. GOVERNMENT SCHOLARSHIP SCHEMES REFERENCED BY CIT
Source: https://cit.edu.in/students/scholarship

A. State Government Scholarships (Tamil Nadu):
1. State Government Scholarship for Backward/Most Backward Classes and Denotified Community students from Tamil Nadu:
   - Eligibility: BC/MBC/DNC Students whose parental annual income is less than Rs. 2,50,000/-.
   - Sanctioning Authority: Principal.
2. Government of India Post Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes/Scheduled Tribe students from Tamil Nadu:
   - Eligibility: SC/ST Students whose parental annual income is less than Rs. 2,50,000/-.
   - Sanctioning Authority: Principal.
3. Adi Dravidar Welfare Higher Education Special Scholarship students from Tamil Nadu:
   - Eligibility: SC/ST Students whose parental annual income is less than Rs. 2,50,000/- (For Hostel Students only).
   - Sanctioning Authority: Principal.
4. State Government Award for Bright Students (Chief Minister's Award) from Tamil Nadu:
   - Eligibility: SC/ST Students who secure the highest marks in the district in the H.S.C Examination.
   - Sanctioning Authority: District Adi Dravidar Welfare Officer, Coimbatore.
5. Teachers' National Welfare Fund Scholarship students from Tamil Nadu:
   - Eligibility: Students whose parents are teachers.
   - Sanctioning Authority: Director of School Education, Chennai-6.
6. Ex-Servicemen's Welfare Scholarship:
   - Eligibility: Children and dependents of Ex-Servicemen.
   - Sanctioning Authority: Secretary / Asst. Director, Ex-Servicemen's Welfare Dept. of the district concerned.
7. State Government Anna Award for Backward / Most Backward / DNC Students from Tamil Nadu:
   - Eligibility: BC/MBC/DNC Students who secure the highest marks in the HSC Examination at district level.
   - Sanctioning Authority: District Backward Classes and Minority Welfare Officer of the district concerned.
8. State Government Gandhi Memorial Award students from Tamil Nadu:
   - Eligibility: SC/ST Students who secure the highest marks in the district in the H.S.C Examination.
   - Sanctioning Authority: Director of Adi Dravidar Welfare, Chennai-5.
9. Tamil Pudhalvan Scheme:
   - Eligibility: Male students who studied from 6th to 12th standard in Government Schools and Government Aided Schools in Tamil Medium in the State.
10. Pudhumai Penn Scheme:
    - Eligibility: Female students who studied from 6th to 12th standard in Government Schools and Government Aided Schools in Tamil Medium in the State.

B. Central Government & Other State Schemes:
1. Prime Minister's Special Scholarship Scheme (PMSSS) for Jammu & Kashmir:
   - Eligibility: Jammu & Kashmir students only; parental annual income below Rs. 6,00,000/-.
2. Online Scholarship Management System (OSMS) Manipur:
   - Eligibility: Scheduled Tribe (ST) students from Manipur; parental annual income below Rs. 2,50,000/-.
3. Other state schemes referenced:
   - Government of Meghalaya Scholarship, Government of Shillong Scholarship, Government of Arunachal Pradesh Scholarship, Government of Tripura Scholarship, Government of Nagaland Scholarship, Government of Mizoram Scholarship, Andaman & Nicobar Scholarship, Umbrella Scheme for Education of ST Children - Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) for ST Students (Arunachal Pradesh - annual income below Rs. 2,00,000/-), Defence Service Scholarship, Tamil Nadu Farmer Social Security Welfare Scholarship.

C. National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Schemes:
1. Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships for College and University Students:
   - Eligibility: BC/MBC/SC/ST/OC students; parental annual income below Rs. 8,00,000/-; 80% marks in the previous year examination.
2. AICTE Pragati Scheme (for Girl Students):
   - Eligibility: Maximum two girl children per family; parental annual income below Rs. 8,00,000/-.
3. Saksham Scheme (for Specially-abled Students):
   - Eligibility: Specially-abled students having a disability of not less than 40%; parental annual income below Rs. 8,00,000/-.
4. Ishan Uday Special Scholarship:
   - Eligibility: General degree, technical, and professional course students from NER; parental annual income below Rs. 4,50,000/-.
5. Post Matric Scholarship for ST Students - Manipur:
   - Eligibility: Scheduled Tribe students of Manipur; parental annual income below Rs. 2,50,000/-.
6. Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme for Ministry of Railways:
   - Eligibility: Wards of RPF/RPSF personnel (Category I, II, III, IV); 60% marks in previous year examination.
7. Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme for Central Armed Police Forces and Assam Rifles:
   - Eligibility: CAPFs/AR personnel wards/dependents (Category A to G); 60% marks in previous year examination.
8. AICTE - Yashasvi Scholarship Scheme:
   - Eligibility: First year of Degree/Diploma level course or second year through lateral entry in AICTE approved institutions; family income not more than Rs. 8,00,000/- per annum.
