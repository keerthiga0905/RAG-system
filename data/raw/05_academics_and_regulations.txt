4. EXAMINATION ADMINISTRATION AND PROCEDURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: https://cit.edu.in/coe/about-the-office-of-the-controller-of-examinations

The Office of the Controller of Examinations (COE) "plays a vital role in
safeguarding the academic credibility of the Institute. It is responsible for
planning, administering, and monitoring all examination-related activities,
ensuring that assessment processes are conducted with fairness, accuracy, and
transparency."

Key responsibilities listed on the page:
- Conduct of Examinations: planning and administering all internal and end-semester
  examinations in accordance with the approved academic schedule for every programme.
- Appointment of Examiners: identifying and appointing competent faculty members and
  external experts for question paper preparation and theory paper evaluation.
- Assessment of Practical and Project Work: constituting specialized Examination
  Committees to evaluate laboratory examinations, project reviews, and viva-voce
  assessments.
- Result Compilation and Declaration: processing, verification and finalization of
  results, followed by official publication after approval by the Result Passing
  Board (RPB).
- Issue of Academic Records: official grade sheets and academic statements.
- Academic Reporting: performance analysis reports presented to the Academic Council.
- Recommendation for Degree Award: verifying eligibility and recommending qualified
  candidates for degrees.
Additional services: certified transcripts and duplicate academic records;
verification of certificates and mark statements; organizing the Annual Graduation Day.
Office administration: headed by the Controller of Examinations, assisted by a Deputy
Controller, supported by thirteen staff members.

Named officials (source: https://cit.edu.in/about-us/autonomous-status): Dr. R. Sriram,
Controller of Examinations; Dr. R. Sathyanarayan Sridhar, Deputy Controller of
Examinations.

6. INTERNAL ASSESSMENT AND END-SEMESTER EXAMINATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Numerical internal/external mark weightage and pass minimums: Not found in the
inspected official sources.

Statements published on https://cit.edu.in/about-us/autonomous-status (no academic year
or regulation version is stated on the page):
- Continuous assessment in laboratory practicals, workshop practice and engineering
  drawing "has been introduced under the Autonomous scheme since 2001."
- "All the question papers for U.G. programmes are set by external/internal examiners,
  and valuation is done both by internal and external examiners."
- "Complete autonomy is given for the conduct of PG programmes. Setting of question
  papers and valuation of answer scripts are done internally."
- "Conduct of examination, publication of results, and issue of grade sheets are done
  systematically and with a fixed time frame."
- Industrial training: "Four weeks of industrial training is compulsory for the
  students to become eligible for the award of a degree by the University. This has
  been in practice since 2009."
- "A mini project of 2 credits is offered to the students of the V and VI semesters."
- Students may study German/French as an alternative to English in the II semester.
