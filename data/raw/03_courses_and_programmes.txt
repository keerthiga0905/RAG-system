- Application process: "The Directorate of Technical Education (DOTE), Chennai will publish admission notifications in leading newspapers. Application forms can be obtained directly from the Institute Office during the notified period."

(Part Time) (2020, 2021, 2022 Batch)", indicating part-time batches exist; no 2026 intake notice for part-time programmes was found in the inspected pages.
- Part-time syllabus links: Not found in the inspected official sources.
- Formatting note: on the post-graduate page the third programme ("Electrical and Electronics Engineering") appears after the first two eligibility bullet points; the UG Admissions page lists all three together.

Eligibility: M.Sc. Software Systems / Data Science / AI and Machine Learning (admission page and M.Sc. Brochure 2026 agree)
  - A pass in HSC (or equivalent) examination with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as subjects is required.
  - Candidates (other than SC/ST) must have a minimum aggregate of 70% in Mathematics + Physics + (Chemistry or Computer Science).
  - Note (as published): this does not mean 70% in each subject individually; the requirement is based on the total combined marks. Example given: out of 300 marks (100 per subject), a minimum of 210 marks in total.

Eligibility: M.Sc. Decision and Computing Sciences
  - A pass in HSC (or equivalent) examination with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as subjects; candidates (other than SC/ST) must have a minimum aggregate of 70% in Mathematics + Physics + (Chemistry or Computer Science)
    (OR)
  - A pass in HSC / equivalent exam with one subject from Category A and two subjects from Category B as the subjects of study.
      CATEGORY A - Mathematics / Applied Mathematics / Statistics / Business Mathematics / Business Mathematics & Statistics.
      CATEGORY B - Commerce / Economics / Accountancy / Computer Science.
    Candidates (other than SC/ST) with an aggregate of 70% and above in the three subjects in total, one from Category A and two from Category B, only need to apply.

Admission process (admission page): two-stage evaluation - (1) marks obtained in the Higher Secondary qualifying board examination; (2) an institute-level entrance examination ("internal entrance test conducted by the institute"). "Note: The examination center is located only in Coimbatore."

11. CONFLICTS, AMBIGUITIES AND FLAGS
================================================================================
C1. B.E. Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology)
    Listed on: homepage; https://cit.edu.in/admission/admission-requirements; https://cit.edu.in/admission/under-graduate; B.E. Brochure 2026 ("Since 2026", "* awaiting approval").
    Absent from: https://cit.edu.in/departments; site navigation menus; contact-form department list; https://cit.edu.in/syllabus.
    Not resolved. The brochure's "awaiting approval" note means the programme should not be treated as an approved, running programme on this evidence alone.

C2. M.E. Construction Management vs M.E. "Civil Engineering"
    https://cit.edu.in/admission/admission-requirements, the homepage and the B.E. Brochure 2026 name "M.E. - Construction Management". https://cit.edu.in/admission/post-graduate lists the M.E. discipline as "Civil Engineering" (four semesters). Eligibility wording also differs between the two pages (quoted in Section 4.1). M.E. Construction Management is absent from https://cit.edu.in/departments and from the site navigation PG list, although it is on the homepage "PG Programmes" block and the Syllabus page. Not resolved. M.E. intake: Not found.

C3. Department naming for the integrated M.Sc. programmes
    https://cit.edu.in/admission/msc-five-year-integrated, the M.Sc. Brochure 2026 and the Software Systems curriculum PDF say "Department of Computing". https://cit.edu.in/departments lists each M.Sc. as its own department entry, and https://cit.edu.in/msc-software-systems says "The Department of Software Systems, established in 1998" while its vision statement begins "Department of Computing endeavors...". Whether there is one department or several is not resolved. The homepage also says "14 departments" while the Departments page shows 18 entries (8 Engineering, 4 M.Sc., 2 MCA/MBA, 4 Science & Humanities). Not resolved.

C4. Category labels for the integrated M.Sc. programmes
    Departments page and site navigation: "PG Programmes". Homepage: "Integrated Programmes" ("Five-Year Integrated Programmes"). Admission Requirements page and M.Sc. Brochure: "Five-year Integrated Programmes". MCA and MBA appear under "Master Programmes" on the Departments page and "PG Programmes" on the homepage and navigation. This is a labelling difference, not a difference of fact.

C5. Syllabus-year label mismatches
    M.Sc. Software Systems: the file in the "2020 - 21" column is named "MSc_Software_2022.pdf"; the department page labels its linked curriculum "Regulation 2022"; the PDF itself says "For the Students admitted during 2020 - 2021 and onwards".
    MCA: the file in the "2020 - 21" column is named "MCA_Syllabus_-_2022_final-cit.pdf".
    B.E. Computer Science and Engineering: the "2019 - 20" file is named "CSE_Syllabus_Final_2022.pdf"; B.Tech Information Technology: the "2019 - 20" file is named "BTech_IT_final_2022.pdf".
    The page header also repeats "2021 - 22" in two columns. Not resolved; the labelled column is reported next to each link.

C6. PG admissions page - minor inconsistencies
    The heading reads "MBA and MBA ADMISSIONS 2026" (the dates beneath it are given for MCA and MBA). The page's meta description mentions "M.Tech programs through TANCET or GATE", but the page body lists no M.Tech programme and does not name TANCET; it mentions GATE only in the M.E. stipend sentence. Entrance-exam names for MBA and MCA: Not found in the inspected official sources.

C7. UG minimum marks for SC/ST
    https://cit.edu.in/admission/admission-requirements says "a mere pass in the qualifying examination will suffice" for SC/ST candidates and also prints "SC/ST - 40% average in the related subjects" in each table. Not resolved.

C8. Duplicate application-form files
    The B.E. / B.Tech. 2026-27 forms linked from https://cit.edu.in/admission/under-graduate and https://cit.edu.in/admission/first-year-be-btech-admissions have different file addresses. Neither was opened, so whether their content differs is unknown.

C9. Brochure layout ambiguities
    In the B.E. Brochure 2026 the M.Sc. Software Systems entry follows the PG programme entries (see Section 1). In the M.Sc. Brochure 2026 the attribution of the two "Specializations" lists to programmes is inferred (see Section 5). In the MBA brochure, "Integrated with Digital Technologies" and "Data Analytics and AI" are printed beside the specialisation list without a stated link to it (see Section 4.2).
