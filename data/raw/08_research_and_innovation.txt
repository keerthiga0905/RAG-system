1. INSTITUTIONAL RESEARCH GOVERNANCE AND RESEARCH HIGHLIGHTS
======================================================================

Source: Research Cell
URL: https://cit.edu.in/research/research-cell

The Research and Development Cell of Coimbatore Institute of Technology promotes research, innovation and academic excellence. It encourages interdisciplinary research and supports scholars undertaking innovative projects.

The R&D Cell assists faculty members in identifying and applying for research grants from government funding agencies and industry sectors. It facilitates collaboration with national and international research organizations and promotes publications, patents and technology development activities. Workshops, seminars and training programs are organized to enhance research capabilities. The Cell also serves as a central platform for managing funded projects and research documentation and motivates students to participate in research-oriented activities.

Research highlights stated on the page:
- Total Publications: 250+ (Journals & Conferences)
- Patents Filed/Granted: 45+
- Funded Projects: 30+ (₹10+ Crores)
- Ongoing Projects: 20+
- Collaborations: 15+ National & International Institutions

These are aggregate figures as displayed on the page. The page does not provide a dated breakdown or a list of every item underlying these totals. Do not interpret these aggregate counts as a verified current status for individual projects, patents or collaborations.

2.2 National Resource Centre (NRC) – Structural Engineering

Source: National Resource Centre (NRC)
URL: https://cit.edu.in/others/nrc

The page describes the National Resource Centre (NRC) for Structural Engineering at CIT as a national-level platform for advanced academic engagement, capacity building and applied research. It states that the centre was established with the support of the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Objectives stated:
- Knowledge sharing on structural analysis, design methodologies and construction materials.
- Faculty capacity building through Annual Refresher Programmes in Teaching (ARPIT), faculty development programmes and domain-specific training workshops.
- Awareness of updated Indian Standards (IS codes) and internationally accepted design practices.
- Development of digital learning resources, recorded lectures and online modules.

Focus areas stated:
- High-performance, self-compacting and advanced concrete materials
- Earthquake engineering, seismic assessment and retrofitting techniques
- Steel–concrete composite systems for modern infrastructure
- Smart materials, sensors and structural health monitoring systems
- Sustainable construction practices and life-cycle assessment
- Computational mechanics, including finite element modeling and AI-based structural performance prediction

Facilities/resources stated:
- Structural Engineering Laboratory, equipped with high-capacity loading systems and advanced testing instruments.
- Digital Content Development Facility for recording online courses, expert lectures and interactive webinars.
- High-Performance Computing Facilities with access to STAAD, SAP2000, ANSYS and ABAQUS.
- Library resources including national and international journals, reference books and updated design standards.

- Examination of interfaces between matrix and reinforcement
- Analysis of pores, voids, cracks, defects and surface irregularities
- Characterization of coatings, thin surface layers and modified surfaces
- Examination of corrosion and degradation-induced surface changes
- Morphological studies of powders, fibres and particulate materials
- Characterization of additively manufactured materials and components
- Comparative analysis of materials before and after processing or surface treatment
