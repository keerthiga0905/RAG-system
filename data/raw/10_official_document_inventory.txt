IMPORTANT SCOPE NOTE
This inventory is a verified inventory of publicly accessible, relevant PDF documents identified by inspecting CIT's official navigation pages and targeted searches of official CIT PDF locations. It is NOT claimed to be a byte-for-byte crawl of every PDF on the entire website. CIT has many department, NAAC criterion, examination, timetable, committee, and announcement attachments; only documents relevant to a College Knowledge AI/RAG corpus and actually identifiable/openable during this inspection are listed below.

STATUS DEFINITIONS
- Opened: YES = the official PDF was successfully opened/parsed by the inspection tool.
- Downloaded: NO = no separate local download was performed; "opened" means the live official PDF was accessible.
- Text: YES = machine-readable text was available from the PDF.
- OCR: REQUIRED = the PDF was image/scanned based or exposed no extractable text during inspection.
- Version/date concern: records known ambiguity, legacy content, stale academic year, or conflicts found in official sources.
