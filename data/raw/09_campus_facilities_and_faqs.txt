E-resources/off-campus access: https://idp.cit.edu.in/
The official CIT library off-campus access page states that faculty members, research scholars and students have credentials for remote access to subscribed e-resources 24/7. Users who have not received login details are directed to library@cit.edu.in. The page lists e-books, databases and e-journals, including McGraw Hill, Scopus, ASCE Journals Online, ASME Journals Online, EBSCO, Elsevier ScienceDirect, IEEE Xplore and Springer.

Library opening hours:
An official PDF titled “LIBRARY” at https://cit.edu.in/uploads/epdf_documents/8e7c826bf2ddd07adbea376368fbf334.pdf publishes the following hours:
- Monday to Friday: 9.00 a.m. – 7.00 p.m.
- Saturday: 9.00 a.m. – 3.00 p.m.
- Vacation: 9.30 a.m. – 4.30 p.m.
- Closed on Sundays and Government holidays.
Caution: this is an older official PDF (search indexing indicates it was crawled several years ago). The currently inspected library pages do not independently confirm these hours. Treat the schedule as historically published, not independently verified as current; contact library@cit.edu.in before relying on it.

5. HOSTEL / STUDENT ACCOMMODATION
Source: https://cit.edu.in/life-at-cit/accommodation
The official accommodation page states:
- Residential facilities are available for aspiring students.
- Hostellers are described as having comfortable living conditions, a healthy diet and comprehensive health care.
- Hostels are within walking distance of academic blocks.
- The page says accommodation is guaranteed for first-time students upon acceptance of their application.
- The Hostel Committee comprises the Principal & Chief Warden, Administrative Officer, Deputy Wardens, Matron and Resident Tutor.
- The listed committee responsibilities include providing information/support to residents, helping with welfare issues, assisting residents’ needs, organising social and sporting events, and encouraging responsible community living.

6. TRANSPORT
Source inspected: https://cit.edu.in/others/sop
The official Standard Operating Procedures page says that for student industrial visits, departments are responsible for arranging authorized transportation, ensuring student insurance coverage and deputing at least two faculty members. For mixed-gender groups, a female faculty member is mandatory.
This concerns industrial visits and does not establish a general daily student bus service.

7. ACCESSIBILITY, SECURITY AND VISITOR INFORMATION
Accessibility source: https://cit.edu.in/general-facilities/
The official General Facilities page states that the institute provides a barrier-free environment, including:
- Lift arrangements in all blocks of the campus
- Ramps in all buildings and laboratories
- Wheelchairs for students who require them
- “Left hand Chairs provided for require students” (wording as published on the page)
- Separate toilets for physically challenged students

Visitor accommodation source: https://cit.edu.in/general-facilities/
The page states that CIT maintains a fully furnished Guest House with modern amenities for visiting professors, industrial experts and parents. Booking process, rates, availability and guest-house contact details were not found in the inspected page.

Security/entry source: https://cit.edu.in/others/sop
The official SOP page says all entry and exit points are monitored continuously and access is permitted after verification of valid identification for students, staff and visitors. Security personnel conduct routine patrols across academic blocks, hostels, laboratories and common areas.
A separate official security document indexed at https://cit.edu.in/uploads/epdf_documents/520554b01edbafd290cf6a3da16208cf.pdf includes internal gate/security instructions. These include recording outsiders without ID cards in registers, restrictions on entry by other college students, and references to students leaving after 7.00 pm and the main gate being closed after evening-class students leave by 9 pm. This appears to be an internal security document and may be time-sensitive; do not treat it as a current public visitor policy without confirmation.

Public visitor registration procedure, visiting hours, parking instructions and a visitor-specific phone number:
Not found in the inspected official sources. Contact the central office or Help Desk before visiting.

Sports facility opening hours, gym access rules, membership charges and a current sports timetable:
Not found in the inspected official sources.

General facilities source: https://cit.edu.in/general-facilities/
The official page lists:
- Canteen facility
- In-house ATM, described as available 24/7
- On-campus health centre with qualified medical staff, first-aid equipment and emergency transport services
- Guest House for visiting professors, industrial experts and parents
- Solar photovoltaic panels
- Centralized Power House with generators and transformers; page describes 24/7 power backup for laboratories, hostels and administrative blocks

- Sewage treatment and wastewater recycling facilities

Academic facilities source: https://cit.edu.in/life-at-cit/academic-facilities
The page lists laboratories, classrooms, auditorium and library. Detailed auditorium booking procedure, seating capacity, access policy and opening hours were not found in the inspected page.

IT/network facilities source: https://cit.edu.in/it-resource-maintenance-cell/laboratory-facilities
The official IT Resource Maintenance Cell page describes a 1 Gbps fiber-optic backbone, wired connectivity, and Wi-Fi. It says Wi-Fi is available in the Main Building Block, IT Block, Library Block and Hostel Office.
